    </div>

    <div class="support-grid">
      <article>
        <h3>Anxiety and overthinking</h3>
        <p>A mind that rarely settles, persistent worry, dread or the feeling that something is about to go wrong.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Low mood and disconnection</h3>
        <p>Feeling flat, distant from yourself, exhausted by ordinary demands or unable to find meaning in familiar things.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Self-criticism and shame</h3>
        <p>A harsh inner voice, perfectionism, difficulty accepting care or a sense that you are always falling short.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Relationships</h3>
        <p>Patterns of withdrawal, conflict, people-pleasing, mistrust or difficulty expressing what you need.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Grief and loss</h3>
        <p>Living with bereavement, separation, a change in identity or the loss of a future you expected to have.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Change and identity</h3>
        <p>Questioning who you are, adjusting to a new stage of life or trying to live with greater honesty and alignment.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>
