      <p class="eyebrow">When insight is not enough</p>
      <h2>You may understand the pattern and still find yourself repeating it.</h2>
    </div>

<div class="prose prose-large">
  <p>You might know why you overthink, withdraw, become defensive or put other people first. That understanding can matter, but it does not always change what happens in the moment when you feel criticised, rejected, overwhelmed or afraid.</p>
  <p>Counselling gives us a chance to notice these responses more closely and with less judgement. My role is to listen carefully, offer reflections, question assumptions where it may help and stay alongside you as you discover what feels true.</p>
  <p>The aim is not a perfect explanation. It is for new understanding to become useful in your life: in the way you speak to yourself, express a need, handle conflict, make a decision or recover after something difficult.</p>
</div>

<section class="section section-border-top" aria-labelledby="uncertainty-title">
  <div class="shell split split-wide">
    <div>
      <p class="eyebrow">What happens in the room</p>
      <h2 id="uncertainty-title">You can bring uncertainty, silence and disagreement.</h2>
    </div>

<div class="prose prose-large">
  <p>If you do not know what to say, we can begin with that. I will not expect you to fill every silence or produce an important insight each week.</p>
  <p>If you go quiet, become emotional, lose your train of thought or move away from a subject, we can notice what is happening without forcing you past your pace.</p>
  <p>If I misunderstand you, I want you to correct me. If a question does not help, you can say so. Counselling should be collaborative enough for disagreement to be part of the work rather than something you have to avoid.</p>
  <p>If you can explain everything intellectually but do not feel any different, we can pay attention to that gap rather than treating more analysis as the answer.</p>
</div>
