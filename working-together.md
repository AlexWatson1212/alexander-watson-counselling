<section class="page-hero shell narrow"><p class="eyebrow">Working together</p><h1>Space to explore, not pressure to perform.</h1><p class="lede">You set the pace. My role is to listen carefully, notice what may be happening between and beneath the words, and help you stay close to what feels important.</p></section>
<section class="shell process-grid">
<article><span>01</span><h2>Initial contact</h2><p>Send a brief message. You do not need to disclose your whole story. We can arrange a short conversation and decide whether it makes sense to continue.</p></article>
<article><span>02</span><h2>First session</h2><p>We explore what has brought you, what you hope for, and what would help the work feel safe and useful.</p></article>
<article><span>03</span><h2>Ongoing work</h2><p>Sessions create continuity: enough space to recognise patterns, experience feelings more fully and develop a different relationship with yourself.</p></article>
</section>
