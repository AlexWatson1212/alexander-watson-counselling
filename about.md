<section class="page-hero shell narrow"><p class="eyebrow">About</p><h1>A thoughtful, direct and human presence.</h1><p class="lede">I am interested in the person beneath the coping strategies: how you learned to survive, what those adaptations have protected, and what might become possible when you no longer have to carry everything in the same way.</p></section>
<section class="shell split prose-section"><div><h2>How I tend to work</h2></div><div><p>I bring curiosity, steadiness and honesty to the room. I will not try to diagnose your personality from a distance or force your experience into a fixed method. Instead, we work together to understand your inner world in a way that feels accurate and useful.</p><p>I value depth, but not unnecessary complexity. Sometimes the most meaningful change begins with finding simple language for something you have felt for years.</p><p class="note"><strong>Professional status:</strong> I am currently training as a counsellor. Final qualification, professional membership and public-practice details must be confirmed before this site goes live.</p></div></section>
