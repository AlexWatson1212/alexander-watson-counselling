<section class="page-hero shell narrow"><p class="eyebrow">Approach</p><h1>Change grows from being understood clearly enough to meet yourself differently.</h1></section>
<section class="shell split prose-section"><div><h2>Person-centred at the core</h2></div><div><p>I believe people change when they have enough safety, honesty and acceptance to encounter their experience without immediately defending against it.</p><p>This does not mean passive listening. It means paying close attention, offering reflections, gently questioning assumptions and staying alongside you as new understanding emerges.</p></div></section>
<section class="section-tint"><div class="shell editorial-grid"><article><span>01</span><h3>Acceptance</h3><p>Your experience does not have to be tidy or reasonable before it deserves attention.</p></article><article><span>02</span><h3>Curiosity</h3><p>We replace judgement with a genuine interest in why your mind and body respond as they do.</p></article><article><span>03</span><h3>Responsibility</h3><p>Understanding the past can create more choice in the present without blaming you for how you learned to survive.</p></article></div></section>
